Who We Are

A technology startup-benefit corporation revolutionizing food preservation and supply chain sustainability through innovative supercooling technology.

SCS Europa SRL SB is a technology startup-benefit corporation founded in November 2024, with headquarters in Bari (Italy), Brussels (Belgium), and Toronto (Canada).

As a member of the Italian National Sea-Economy Technology Cluster, SCS focuses on innovative methods for preserving perishable goods, including fruits, vegetables, fish, and meat.

Their main innovation, the SuperCooling System uses a low-intensity electric field to slow degradation without dehydration or chemicals, preventing ice crystal formation. This method enhances food safety, freshness, and sensory quality while reducing waste and promoting supply-chain sustainability.

Intelligent Cold Chain as a Service

Transforming traditional logistics with AI-powered preservation technology

📦❄️
ICCAAS

SCS Europa specializes in developing, piloting, and commercializing advanced preservation solutions for perishable products through a service-oriented business model.

Leveraging proprietary SuperCooling technology integrated into standard ISO refrigerated containers, enhanced with IoT sensors and AI software, we transform passive containers into intelligent assets.

This Intelligent Cold Chain as a Service (ICCAAS) model enables real-time tracking, automated alerts, and insightful dashboards—positioning our offering as much more than a hardware solution.

Real-Time Intelligence

Advanced IoT sensors provide continuous monitoring of temperature, humidity, and cargo conditions with predictive analytics throughout the entire supply chain journey.

AI-Powered Optimization

Machine learning algorithms continuously optimize preservation conditions and predict potential issues before they occur, ensuring maximum efficiency.

Service-First Model

Flexible subscription and usage-based pricing makes advanced preservation technology accessible to businesses of all sizes without heavy upfront investment.

Waste Reduction

SCS Europe differentiates itself by marrying disruptive SuperCooling technology with IoT, AI, and data services, in a scalable "servitization" model that shifts clients from CAPEX to predictable OPEX.

The result: extended shelf life (often exceeding 40%), reduced spoilage, superior product quality, and lower logistics emissions. Our solution enables higher cargo density (eliminating ice load), modal shifts to more sustainable transport modes, and supports traceable ESG compliance across environment, social impact, and governance dimensions.

As a member of Cluster BIG, we help European food logistics become smarter and greener—delivering measurable impact from product origin to consumer's table.
